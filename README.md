## What is Oriens?

Oriens is a brand crafting fully customizable mousepads. Every pad is built by hand, combining carefully sourced materials with a level of precision and passion that mass production can't match.

## The Atelier

The **Atelier** is the configurator at the heart of Oriens. It lets you compose your ideal mousepad step by step — choosing the surface fabric, base, dimensions, and edge finishing — before submitting your order.

Read the full guide → [docs/atelier.md](docs/atelier.md)
