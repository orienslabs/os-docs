# Contributing to Oriens

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Oriens project. Here is how you can get involved.

---

## Ways to Contribute

- **Report a bug** — open an issue describing what you found
- **Suggest a feature or improvement** — open an issue with your idea
- **Improve documentation** — fix typos, clarify explanations, add missing info

---

## Before You Start

1. Check if there is already an open issue for what you want to report or suggest.
2. Be respectful and constructive in all interactions.
3. For larger proposals, open an issue first to discuss before putting in the work.

---

## Reporting a Bug

When opening a bug report, please include:

- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Browser and operating system (if relevant)

---

## Suggesting a Feature

When suggesting a new feature or improvement, please describe:

- The problem you are trying to solve
- Your proposed solution
- Any alternatives you considered

---

## Questions?

Join the community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/oriens) — it is the best place to ask questions, share feedback, and stay up to date with what is coming.
