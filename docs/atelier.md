# The Atelier — Configurator Guide

The Atelier is the core feature of Oriens. It is a step-by-step wizard that guides you through every choice that makes up your mousepad — from the surface fabric all the way to the edge finish.

Access to the Atelier is by invitation only. Once you have a valid access code, you can begin your configuration.

---

## Step 1 — Surface

Choose the top fabric of your mousepad. There are 11 surface options, each with distinct glide characteristics and a category:

| Category | Feel |
|----------|------|
| Speed | Fast, low-friction glide |
| Control | High stopping power, precise tracking |
| Hybrid | Balanced between speed and control |
| Comfort | Soft, cushioned, ideal for long sessions |

Each surface has a unique weave structure (e.g. cashmere, twill, spacer knit, satin) that determines how your mouse behaves on it.

---

## Step 2 — Base

Choose the rubber base layer. The base determines how the pad feels underfoot and how much cushion and rebound it provides.

| Base | Material | Hardness | Rebound |
|------|----------|----------|---------|
| CR-45° | CR Rubber Sponge | 45° ± 5 | 6/10 |
| G-15° | Polyethylene Foam | 15° ± 5 | 8/10 |
| AR-102E | Polyolefin Foam | 24° ± 5 | 9/10 |
| CR-30° | CR Rubber Sponge | 25° ± 5 | 8/10 |

You also select the **thickness**: `4mm` (standard) or `6mm` (extra cushion).

> Hover over each base card to see a close-up texture detail.

---

## Step 3 — Dimensions

Set the size of your pad in millimeters. The default is `450 × 400 mm`. You can adjust both width and height freely.

---

## Step 4 — Edge

Choose how the edges of your pad are finished:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Rounded Edge** | Clean cut with softened corners — minimal and sleek |
| **Cover Edge** | Fabric folds underneath for a seamless, premium finish |
| **Stitched Edge** | Reinforced thread border — maximum durability, with a custom color |

If you choose **Stitched Edge**, you will be asked to pick a thread color in the next step.

---

## Step 5 — Stitching Color *(Stitched Edge only)*

Pick the color of the stitched border from 18 available thread colors, including white, black, navy, red, pink, purple, and more.

---

## Step 6 — Summary & Review Unit Request

The final step shows a full recap of your configuration. Since the Atelier is currently in review unit mode, there is no purchase price — instead, you fill in your shipping information and submit a request.

**Required information:**
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Street address, city, postcode, country
- Special note (optional — e.g. packaging preferences)

Once submitted, your request is received by the Oriens team and you will be contacted directly.
